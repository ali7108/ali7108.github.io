Projects

Mole-Inspired Drilling Robot

Bio-inspired drilling robot concept for mining applications, based on the burrowing behaviour of a mole.

  • Expandable drill bit that opens from the body while rotating, targeting a 350 mm bore
  • Linear actuator used to change left/right direction
  • Triangular chain drive supporting movement through the bore
  • Primary materials: Aluminum 7075 and steel; many components designed as sheet-metal parts suitable for laser cutting
  • Full exploded technical drawing and rendered assembly produced

Overhead Lifting Crane Gearbox

High-reduction transmission concept for controlled lifting and heavy-duty material handling.

  • Overall reduction ratio of 1:284
  • High-strength alloy steel specified for gears, intended for CNC machining
  • Encoder at the output shaft to monitor position, speed and direction
  • Transducer at the motor shaft to convert mechanical torque into an electrical signal for monitoring
  • Full assembly breakdown with bill of materials and engineering drawing

Complete Mountain Bike Assembly

Full SolidWorks model built for mechanical accuracy and high-quality visualisation — frame, suspension, drivetrain and wheels.

  • Role: Lead Product Designer and 3D Modeler
  • 12-speed drivetrain and gear-shifter assembly, individually modelled chain components
  • Complete exploded view documentation of frame and drivetrain
  • Final advertising-quality renders produced for a bicycle manufacturing/marketing context

Pagani Huayra — Surface Modelling Study

Advanced SolidWorks surfacing project focused on complex automotive form and aerodynamic exterior geometry.

  • Complex exterior curves and wind-sculpted body form built using SolidWorks surfacing tools
  • Challenge: capturing the vehicle's visual character while maintaining a coherent, technically controlled surface model
  • Multiple photorealistic renders produced with applied materials and textures

WiFi-Controlled 3D-Printed RC Jeep In Progress

Independent build: a custom RC vehicle designed from scratch in SolidWorks and 3D printed, controlled over WiFi from a phone browser.

  • Rear differential with diff lock and custom bevel gear sets
  • Geared steering knuckle with 10° kingpin inclination, requiring non-orthogonal (80°) bevel gear pairs
  • ESP32-based control system handling combined steering and throttle over a self-hosted web server
  • Iterative structural redesign of the steering knuckle to address stress concentration, including split-part printing with interlocking joints
